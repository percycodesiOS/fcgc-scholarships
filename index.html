Review, download, and submit

The downloaded PDF includes the cover letter, your answers, and calculated totals. It can be viewed in a phone's email preview without needing to run calculations.

Include with your application

  • The first two pages of your FAFSA summary, if submitted.
  • Your school's financial aid offer/award letter or acceptance letter; a financial aid estimate if available. Explain unavailable documents.
  • One recommendation from someone outside Fox Chapel Golf Club. Letters from FCGC personnel do not qualify.
  • Your personal essay: 1 or 2 pages about your educational and occupational goals and how this scholarship would help.
  • A digital photo emailed to wparks@foxchapelgolfclub.com.
  • Student signature and, if under 18, parent or guardian signature. Typed names here are not certified digital signatures; follow the Foundation's signature requirements.

Submit the application and required documents to the Scholarship Committee, Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation, Inc., 426 Fox Chapel Road, Pittsburgh, PA 15238.

Deadline: Friday, June 18, 2027, at 4:30 p.m. EDT. Missing documents will nullify your application unless explained. The Foundation will not follow up.

This scholarship is not an employee bonus. Applying does not guarantee an interview or a scholarship. An interview does not guarantee a scholarship.

Questions: Call the Club front office or email scholarshipfoundation@foxchapelgolfclub.com.
